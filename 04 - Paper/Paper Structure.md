# Paper Structure

## Status

Structure only. **No substantive paper prose is included.**

The required structure is derived directly from the assessment form and module-book assignment.

## Required Front Matter

### Title

- Purpose: identify the study clearly and appropriately.
- Requirement: appropriate title; title/MRQ/SQs require lecturer approval before writing.
- Status: current title Proposed / approval To Verify.

### Concise Abstract / Summary

- Required by form-aspects gate.
- B1 requires it to be short/concise and cover introduction/method, results and conclusion.
- Draft only after results and conclusions exist.

## Required Main Sections

### 1. Introduction

Must cover the B2 assessment rows:

- reason for investigation;
- description/explanation of subject;
- social importance;
- MRQ, three SQs and objective(s);
- scope;
- structure overview/reading guidance;
- logical connection among these elements.

### 2. Method

Must cover the B3 rows:

- population and sample;
- data collection;
- instruments;
- analysis;
- argumentation of methodological choices.

For the current **Proposed** literature-based investigation, the repository plans publication/evidence population and screened sample, search/screening/extraction instruments and comparative/thematic document analysis. This operationalisation is not official school wording and remains subject to lecturer/assessor acceptance.

### 3. Results

Must satisfy B4:

- claims substantiated/argued and consistent;
- authors' own input evident and existing literature linked to own research;
- informative;
- sufficient depth;
- adequate source amount, completeness, timeliness, quality and relevance.

Planned organisation by research question:

- 3.1 Detection techniques — SQ1;
- 3.2 Performance under operationally defined real-world conditions — SQ2;
- 3.3 Factors reducing effectiveness — SQ3.

This is a logical drafting structure, not an additional school requirement.

### 4. Conclusions and Discussion

Must satisfy B5:

- answer MRQ and SQs;
- be readable as a standalone part;
- remain consistent with results;
- contain identifiable recommendations;
- evaluate methodological results for reliability, validity and usability.

No new unsupported evidence should appear here.

## Required Back Matter

### Literature List / References

- Required by form-aspects gate.
- Assessed for APA under A.
- Official APA edition not stated in available school documents.

## Figures and Tables

The form gate explicitly checks whether text is supported by figures and tables that are correctly deployed/referenced. The safest plan is to include only evidence-bearing synthesis visuals that materially support the results.

## Appendices

No paper appendix is explicitly mandatory in the available sources. Supporting research-process appendices may be useful if Teams rules permit them. Assessment-critical argumentation must remain in the paper itself.

## Explicitly Not Mandated by Available Paper Sources

No separate:

- theoretical-framework chapter;
- executive summary;
- preface;
- recommendations chapter separate from B5;
- professional product.

These should not be added merely from generic academic convention.

## Page-Volume Rule

The assessment form requires 8–10 pages and its footnote says this count applies to the `body` while excluding introduction, method, conclusion, discussion and source list.

**To Verify:** exact total-document interpretation and abstract treatment. Do not convert this wording into an invented word count.
