# Paper Structure

## Status

Structure only. **No substantive paper prose is included.**

The structure is derived from the official assessment form and module-book research-paper assignment.

## Required Front Matter

### Title

Purpose: identify the study clearly and appropriately.  
Requirements: appropriate title; lecturer approval before writing according to the module book.  
Status: current title Proposed / approval To Verify.

### Concise Abstract / Summary

Purpose: provide a short standalone overview.  
Required content from B1: introduction/method, results and conclusion.  
Draft timing: write after research and conclusions exist.

## Required Chapters / Sections

### 1. Introduction

Purpose:

- explain the reason for the investigation;
- describe/explain the subject;
- establish social importance;
- state main question, three subquestions and objectives;
- define scope;
- provide structure/reading guidance;
- maintain logical connection between these elements.

Evidence required: contextual and source-backed justification plus approved research questions.

### 2. Method

Purpose: make the investigation transparent and justified.

Required elements from B3:

- population and sample;
- data collection;
- instruments;
- analysis;
- argumentation of methodological choices.

For the planned structured literature review, these become candidate-source population, included-study sample, search/screening/extraction process, analytical synthesis and rationale.

### 3. Results

Purpose: present evidence and authors' synthesis that directly addresses the subquestions.

Required qualities:

- claims substantiated/argued and consistent;
- authors' own input visible;
- existing literature linked to own research;
- informative and sufficiently deep;
- sources sufficient in amount, completeness, timeliness, quality and relevance.

Planned logical organisation:

- 3.1 Techniques used for deepfake detection (SQ1)
- 3.2 Detection performance under defined real-world conditions (SQ2)
- 3.3 Factors reducing effectiveness (SQ3)

Exact subsection names may be refined after Phase 2 evidence collection.

### 4. Conclusions and Discussion

Purpose:

- answer main and subquestions;
- provide a coherent standalone synthesis;
- state identifiable recommendations;
- discuss limitations and methodological quality;
- evaluate reliability, validity and usability of the research/results.

Do not introduce unsupported new evidence here.

## Required Back Matter

### Literature List / References

- Must correspond with citations.
- APA required.
- Official APA edition not named in uploaded school documents; To Verify.

## Figures and Tables

The form-aspects gate expects the text to be supported by figures and tables that are correctly used/referenced. They should be evidence-bearing, not decorative.

## Appendices

No research-paper appendix is explicitly mandatory in the uploaded school documents. Appendices may be useful for detailed search/screening evidence if allowed by the submission rules. Any appendix strategy must not be used to evade the page constraint.

## Page-Volume Rule

Assessment form: 8–10 pages for the body, with the footnote excluding introduction, method, conclusion, discussion and source list from that body count.

**To Verify:** treatment of abstract and exact total-paper implication.
