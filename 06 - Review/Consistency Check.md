# Consistency Check

## Future Consistency Checklist

Before submission, verify consistency across:

- problem statement;
- research objective;
- main research question;
- exactly three subquestions;
- terminology (`deepfake`, `AI-generated`, `manipulated`, `detector`, `reliability`, `real-world conditions`);
- modality labels (image, video, audio);
- time scope / meaning of `current`;
- methodology wording and actual procedure;
- population/sample counts;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- dataset and benchmark names;
- metric labels/definitions;
- reported values and units;
- figures/tables vs source data;
- results vs conclusions;
- recommendations vs findings;
- citations vs references;
- dates and versions;
- title wording across submission files.

The assessment form states `8–10 pages` and its footnote says this applies to the body while excluding introduction, method, conclusion, discussion and source list. It does not explicitly say whether the abstract is inside/outside the count.

### 3. Submission date

The assessment form front page displays Friday, 25 September 2026. The module book says final paper submission is Friday of week 4. Treat the exact date as **To Verify** until Teams schedule/instructions confirm the connection.

### 4. Typographical wording in school documents

The assessment form/module book contain occasional typographical or translation inconsistencies (for example variations around unassessable codes and legacy wording). Preserve the substantive requirements rather than copying obvious typographical noise into the paper.
