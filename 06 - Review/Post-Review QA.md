## Method integrity check

The largest methodological change is transparency rather than retrospective reconstruction.

The revised method now states:

- the literature-study rationale;
- the search-term families used;
- the 2019-2026 publication window;
- inclusion/exclusion logic;
- the extraction approach;
- the fact that exact search systems, complete search strings and found/excluded counts were not systematically logged.

This is intentionally consistent with section 4.4. The paper no longer implies a higher level of reproducibility than the research process actually supports.

## Layout QA

The revised DOCX was rendered to page images and visually checked page by page.

Result:

- 20 rendered pages;
- no clipped text;
- no overlapping text or figures;
- no broken tables;
- no missing glyphs;
- captions remain with their figures/tables;
- headings and page breaks remain readable.

The automatically generated table of contents in the DOCX can display stale page numbers in a headless renderer after edits. In Microsoft Word, update fields before final submission (`Ctrl+A`, then `F9`) so the TOC reflects the revised pagination.
