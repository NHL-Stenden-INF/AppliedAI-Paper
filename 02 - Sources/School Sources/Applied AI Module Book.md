## Purpose

Defines the module learning outcomes, assessment architecture, period-1 Go/no-go, portfolio evidence, schedule and Appendix 3 research-paper assignment.

## Research-Paper Requirements

Appendix 3 (printed pp. 41–42 / PDF pp. 47–48) requires:

- paper written in pairs during the first four weeks;
- topic related to Applied AI;
- initial proposal containing topic, provisional title, one MRQ and three SQs;
- proposed title/MRQ/SQs discussed with and approved by a lecturer before writing starts;
- relevant/reliable sources, coherent argument, evidence-based answers and clear separation between source information, own analysis and conclusions;
- peer review during week 3;
- reviewer feedback containing substantive strengths and improvement suggestions;
- authors to consider received feedback and improve where appropriate;
- evidence of received and provided feedback included in the portfolio;
- final paper submitted Friday of week 4 according to Teams instructions;
- paper assessed Sufficient/Insufficient;
- successful paper mandatory for period-1 Go/no-go.

## Portfolio / Go-No-Go Relevance

Appendix 1 (printed p. 33 / PDF p. 39) confirms period-1 portfolio evidence includes:

- completed research paper;
- substantive peer feedback received;
- substantive/constructive peer feedback provided;
- evidence showing how received feedback was processed where relevant.

Appendix 8 (printed pp. 55–56 / PDF pp. 61–62) confirms the research paper is one of three mandatory Go/no-go elements and that the elements cannot compensate for each other.

## Learning-Outcome Relevance

The module book defines six learning outcomes and maps them primarily to project contribution, learning path, professional conduct and portfolio evidence. It does **not** explicitly map the period-1 research paper to a specific learning outcome subset.
