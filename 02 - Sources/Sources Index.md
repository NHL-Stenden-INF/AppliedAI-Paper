## Source-Use Rules for Phase 2

- Every external research source receives a stable ID such as `SRC-A001`.
- Every important source is linked to one or more research questions and Evidence Matrix IDs.
- Evidence directness and source quality are assessed separately.
- Review papers may establish field structure and context; major performance claims should preferably be traceable to primary empirical evidence where feasible.
- Preprints, vendor material and technical documentation may be useful for currency or implementation detail, but their role and limitations must be labelled explicitly.
- Missing metadata must be recorded as `Unknown`; never invent author, year, DOI, publisher or URL.

## Source Categories

School, Academic, Professional, Project, Internal, Government, Standard, Dataset, Interview, Observation, Technical Documentation, Other.

## Reliability Principle

A source is not `strong` merely because it is relevant. Assess authority/publication context, currency, independence where relevant, method/evidence basis, transparency, directness to the intended claim, limitations and relevance.
