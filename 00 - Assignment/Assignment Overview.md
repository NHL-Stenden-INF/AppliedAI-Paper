# Assignment Overview

## Assignment

During the first four weeks of the Applied AI module, students write an academic research paper in pairs on a topic related to Applied AI. The paper must investigate the chosen topic in a structured, evidence-based way and communicate the findings clearly.

The current project-specific proposal concerns **AI-generated deepfakes and the technology used to detect them**, with the working title **The reliability of AI-based deepfake detection**.

## Academic Purpose

The school states that the assignment is intended to develop the ability to:

- investigate an Applied AI topic in a structured and evidence-based manner;
- use relevant and reliable sources appropriately;
- build a coherent argument;
- answer research questions on the basis of collected evidence;
- distinguish source-based information from the authors' own analysis and conclusions;
- communicate findings clearly in an academic paper.

## Expected Deliverables

For the research-paper assignment:

1. An initial proposal containing a topic, provisional title, one main research question and three subquestions.
2. A lecturer-approved title, main research question and subquestions before paper writing starts.
3. A research paper written in pairs.
4. A substantive peer review received during week 3 and demonstrably considered.
5. Substantive and constructive peer feedback provided to at least one other student's paper.
6. The final paper submitted on Friday of week 4 following Teams instructions.
7. Evidence of the paper and both received and provided peer feedback included in the period-1 portfolio.

## Assessment Method

The research paper is assessed as **Sufficient or Insufficient** using the prescribed research-paper assessment form. Successful completion is mandatory for the period-1 Go/no-go assessment.

Before substantive assessment, every form-aspect item must be present/met. If required components are missing, the assessment form states that the paper is not assessable.

## Major Requirements

- Pair assignment.
- Applied AI topic.
- Lecturer approval of title and research questions before writing.
- One main research question and exactly three subquestions in the proposal.
- Relevant and reliable sources.
- Coherent evidence-based argumentation.
- Clear distinction between sources, own analysis and conclusions.
- Required structural components: concise abstract, introduction, method, results, conclusions/discussion, literature list.
- Appropriate title, target-group language, design, spelling/grammar, paragraph structure and internal consistency.
- APA must be applied.
- Figures and tables must support the text and be correctly deployed/referenced.
- Volume requirement: **8–10 pages**. The assessment-form footnote states that this count applies to the **body** and excludes the introduction, method, conclusion, discussion and source list. The treatment of the abstract is not stated and must be verified.

## Required Research

The uploaded school materials do not prescribe one specific research design for this paper. They do, however, assess whether the method clearly describes:

- population and sample;
- data collection;
- instruments;
- analysis;
- argumentation of methodological choices.

For the current topic, the planned approach is a structured literature review with comparative synthesis. This is a **Proposed** methodology, not an executed method and not yet represented as final paper prose.

## Required Professional Output

No separate professional product is required as part of this research-paper assignment. The wider Applied AI module includes project and portfolio outputs, but those are distinct from the period-1 research paper.

## Required Validation

The research-paper rubric explicitly expects the discussion to evaluate:

- reliability;
- validity;
- usability.

The exact validation design must be appropriate to the chosen research method.

## Required Reflection

No standalone reflection chapter is specified for the research paper. Peer feedback must be considered, and the discussion must evaluate methodological quality. Broader professional reflection belongs to the module portfolio rather than this paper.
