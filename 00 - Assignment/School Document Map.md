## Authority Model

Default precedence used for this project:

1. official assessment rubric / assessment form;
2. official assignment brief;
3. official programme or module requirements;
4. explicit assignment-specific written instruction;
5. official methodology/research guidance;
6. official template;
7. formal lecturer/supervisor feedback;
8. lecture material;
9. examples;
10. informal notes.

Specificity, date/version and whether an instruction explicitly supersedes another instruction are also considered.

## Why Each Document Matters

### SRC-S01 — Research-paper assessment form

This is the strongest source for what must be present in the paper and what examiners will judge. Page 4 contains the form-aspects gate and page-volume requirement. Page 5 contains the substantive A and B1–B5 assessment criteria.

### SRC-S02 — Applied AI module book

This is the strongest source for the assignment process and its place in the module. Appendix 3 defines the period-1 research-paper assignment; Appendix 1 specifies portfolio evidence; Appendix 8 confirms the paper as a mandatory Go/no-go element.

### SRC-P01 — Research paper subject sheet

This is not a school rule source. It is the project-specific source of the current deepfake-detection topic and research-question wording. Because the module requires lecturer approval before writing and no approval evidence is present, the recorded questions remain **Proposed / To Verify** rather than Confirmed.
