## Traceability Breaks Requiring Attention

1. **Lecturer approval:** source requirement exists, but approval evidence is absent.
2. **Page counting:** requirement exists, but the exact total-document interpretation is ambiguous.
3. **B3 literature-method fit:** a credible document-analysis route exists, but school sources do not explicitly confirm that `population/sample` may be operationalised as publication population/sample.
4. **B4 evidence:** no external research evidence exists yet by design; Phase 2 must populate the evidence path.
5. **Teams-only rules:** referenced but unavailable, so final submission traceability is incomplete.

## Reverse-Traceability Rule

Every Phase-2 research activity must support at least one research question, B3/B4 evidence need or assessment-critical quality control. Activities without a clear requirement or research purpose should not be added.
