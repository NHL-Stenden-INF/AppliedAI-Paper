## Audit Conclusion

The foundation itself is now structurally ready to collect evidence. The dominant gaps are substantive Phase-2 research gaps, not missing repository templates.

The most important evidence gap is a defensible distinction between **controlled benchmark performance** and **generalisation/robustness under explicitly defined conditions**. Without that distinction, SQ2 and the MRQ cannot be answered responsibly.
