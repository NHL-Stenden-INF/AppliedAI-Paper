## Main Research Gaps for Phase 2

The foundation contains **no substantive academic deepfake-detection evidence yet**. Phase 2 must therefore establish the full external evidence base before paper drafting begins.

The highest-value research gap is not simply `find sources`; it is finding evidence that can distinguish controlled benchmark performance from reliability under changed datasets, compression, unseen generators and deliberate evasion.
