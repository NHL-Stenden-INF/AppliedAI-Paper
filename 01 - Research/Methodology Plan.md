# Methodology Plan

## Status

**Proposed / Planned. No research has been executed.**

The school does not prescribe one named method for the period-1 paper. The assessment form requires a method section that describes population/sample, data collection, instruments, analysis and the rationale for methodological choices.

## Research Approach

**Proposed:** structured literature review / desk research with comparative and thematic synthesis.

Rationale:

- the current research questions ask what techniques exist, how accurately systems perform, and which factors reduce effectiveness;
- these questions can be addressed with existing empirical research and technical evidence;
- no primary-data or software implementation requirement appears in the uploaded school documents;
- the method can be made transparent enough to satisfy the B3 assessment criteria.

## Research Strategy

Use a reproducible evidence-selection process rather than an informal narrative source collection:

1. define terms and eligibility;
2. document searches;
3. screen candidates;
4. assess source quality/relevance;
5. extract structured evidence;
6. synthesise by research question;
7. report limitations, reliability, validity and usability.

## Method 1 — Structured Literature Review

### Purpose

Build the evidence base for all three subquestions.

### Research Questions Addressed

SQ1, SQ2 and SQ3.

### Data or Evidence Needed

- primary empirical deepfake-detection studies;
- high-quality recent reviews for landscape mapping and cross-checking;
- benchmark/evaluation studies with explicit datasets, metrics and conditions;
- robustness/generalisation studies relevant to compression, unseen generators or evasion.

### Population and Sampling Approach

For B3 purposes:

- **Population:** potentially relevant scholarly/technical publications concerning AI-based deepfake detection within the defined scope.
- **Sample:** publications that pass the final inclusion/exclusion criteria after title/abstract and full-text screening.

**Planned inclusion criteria:** direct relevance to one or more research questions; sufficient methodological detail; identifiable evaluation basis; credible publication/source context; usable evidence about technique, performance or limitations.

**Planned exclusion criteria:** sources focused only on generation without detection relevance; unsupported commentary; duplicated evidence; papers outside final scope; sources without enough information to assess the claimed result.

Exact year/language/source-type criteria are **To Verify** during Phase 2 setup.

### Planned Procedure

- Record search date, query, search system and result count.
- Remove duplicates.
- Screen titles/abstracts.
- Review full text for eligibility and evidence quality.
- Record inclusion/exclusion decisions for important borderline cases.
- Extract evidence into a standard matrix.
- Cross-check extracted values and interpretations between both authors for important sources.

### Instruments

Planned instruments:

- search protocol;
- inclusion/exclusion checklist;
- source assessment template;
- Evidence Matrix;
- extraction fields for modality, method, dataset/evaluation context, metric, reported result, robustness/generalisation condition, limitation and source quality.

### Planned Analysis

- **SQ1:** thematic/taxonomic synthesis of detection approaches, separated by modality where useful.
- **SQ2:** comparative synthesis of reported performance with the evaluation context preserved. Numerical aggregation is used only when metrics and conditions are genuinely comparable.
- **SQ3:** thematic synthesis of factors that degrade detection performance, with supporting empirical evidence.
- **MRQ:** integrative judgement based on the consistency, strength and limitations of evidence across SQ1–SQ3.

### Reliability Considerations

- use documented eligibility rules;
- cross-check source selection and high-impact extractions between both authors;
- preserve exact metric/context rather than transcribing isolated percentages;
- keep a research log so search and selection decisions are reproducible.

### Validity Considerations

- define `reliable` and `real-world conditions` before extraction;
- avoid generalising benchmark results beyond their test setting;
- seek evidence across modalities and conditions represented by the research questions;
- distinguish in-domain performance from cross-dataset/unseen-generator robustness;
- treat source quality and methodological limitations as part of the conclusion.

### Limitations

Expected limitations include publication bias, heterogeneous datasets/metrics, fast-changing generative models, incomplete comparability across image/video/audio, and limited time for exhaustive review.

### Required Preparation

- lecturer approval status confirmed;
- operational definitions;
- search queries;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- extraction fields;
- source-quality rules.
